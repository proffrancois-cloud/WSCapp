Multiplayer campus

A lightweight WSC study campus for rooms, chat, and movement.

The WSCapp campus lets students meet in room-scoped spaces, move avatars, chat publicly, and enter Debate Lab without loading every player globally.

Room scoped

Players only receive the state for the room they are currently in.

Smooth movement

The realtime layer is designed for compact movement updates and grouped snapshots.

Debate ready

Debate Lab can use signaling for limited audio while keeping audio peer-to-peer.

WSCapp is a free unofficial study app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by World Scholar's Cup.
